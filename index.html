Turn-based tactics

Every turn counts

Two squads meet on a grid. Initiative decides who goes first. On your turn you have 60 seconds to move, attack and defend: all three in one turn.

  • Move

    Cross the field. Rocks, trees and living foes stand in your way.

  • Attack

    Strike up close or shoot from range. Aim at the head, body or legs.

  • Defend

    Raise your guard over one part of the body until your next turn.

Every item adds to your hero's final stats

  • Hit Points how long you last on the battlefield

  • Damage how much HP the enemy loses per hit

  • Protection how well each part of your body is protected

  • Distance the range of your attack

  • Initiative your place in the turn order

  • Dexterity chance to avoid taking damage

  • Critical Hit Chance chance to deal double damage

  • Speed how far you can move on the field

On-chain Next page

On-chain loot

Your gear. Your wallet.

Every helm, blade and cloak you earn is a real token. Fight with it in the arena, take it to your wallet, bring it back whenever you like.

  • Items are NFTs

    Withdraw any item as an ERC-1155 token, trade it, deposit it back to equip it again.

  • Packs and rarities

    Open packs on-chain with a fair draw. Five rarities, from Common to Legendary.

  • Stakes in the arena

    The lobby host sets the entry price. The winning squad takes the pot.

  • Runs on Robinhood Chain

    Items and packs live on an Ethereum layer 2: fast transactions, small fees in ETH.
